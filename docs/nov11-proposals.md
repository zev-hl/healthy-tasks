# HL Central Tasks: proposed changes from the Nov 11 meeting

**Status: for review. Nothing here is built.** Each item is a proposal to confirm, change or drop.
Once the set is agreed, it becomes an implementation plan.

**Sources**

- The Nov 11 meeting (Lipa, Zev, Shloma): Gemini's notes, and the 33 frames from the recording
  in that document. The word-for-word transcript and the video weren't available.
- Lipa's ticket app: its source code (`ticket-brecx`, a rewrite of the live Syruvia) and
  yesterday's walkthroughs of ticket.brecx.com.
- Brecx Connect: its source code. connect.brecx.com itself couldn't be opened from here.
- HL Central as it is now: the brief's redesign. The meeting changes built on Nov 11–12 were
  rolled back on Nov 13.

**How to read it**

- **Meeting:** decided or asked for in the meeting ("Aligned", or a next step).
- **Vision:** Lipa's design approach as shown in the meeting, not a decision.
- **Work:** FE = HL Central screens only; BE = HL Central server and database; CX = Connect,
  which needs Connect's owner.
- **Q** = a question to settle before building.

**The rule from the last review:** the meeting decides, and Lipa's ticket app only shows what
she meant. Where her app doesn't match the meeting, the meeting wins.

## K. Connect integration

What Connect already offers to other apps (its "bot" API, with a key an Admin creates in
Connect):

- **Messages:** send to a person or a group chat.
- **Group chats:** create one and add people.
- **Calls:** book a call, or ring people right now ("Connect rings them"); each gets a join link.
- **Meeting links.**
- **Embed:** a script that adds Connect's chat as a pop-up panel to another `*.brecx.com` site.

**Missing on Connect's side:**

- telling another app when something happens (no webhooks or events);
- listing a person's calls across the workspace;
- opening the embedded panel on a specific chat;
- embedding from sites outside `brecx.com`.

| #   | Proposal                                                                                                                                                   | Source                                            | Work                                                                                        |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| K1  | **Connect's chat on every HL Central page,** as the small pop-up from its embed script, so chat is one click away and tied to tasks.                       | Meeting (Shloma: chat interconnected, as a popup) | FE + CX (domain)                                                                            |
| K2  | **"Schedule call" on a task:** book a Connect call with the assignee (or others), at a time or now. The join link and the call are saved on the task (D8). | Meeting                                           | BE + FE (uses Connect's API as is)                                                          |
| K3  | **Task events as Connect messages:** assigned, mentioned, request update, due, overdue, back from snooze; each person chooses in their settings.           | Meeting (notifications)                           | BE (uses Connect's API as is)                                                               |
| K4  | **Connect calls in HL Central's calendar.**                                                                                                                | Meeting                                           | CX: needs "a person's calls" from Connect, or HL Central shows only calls booked from tasks |
| K5  | **"Discuss in Connect" on a task:** opens (or creates) a group chat for the task.                                                                          | Meeting (chat mirrors tickets)                    | BE + FE + CX (deep link into the panel)                                                     |
| K6  | **Creating a task from a Connect chat or voice note.**                                                                                                     | Meeting (Lipa)                                    | CX (Connect side) + BE (HL Central API key)                                                 |

**Q-K1. Who owns users and permissions?** The meeting decided HL Central creates users and
passes permissions to the other tools. Connect and Lipa's app already follow a different central
service, **auth.brecx.com** (Brecx accounts), which can add, change and remove people in them.
Options:

- (a) HL Central feeds auth.brecx.com;
- (b) HL Central signs in through auth.brecx.com and reads people from it;
- (c) HL Central pushes people straight to Connect.

This is a decision for Zev and Connect's owner.
**Q-K2.** Is HL Central going to live under `*.brecx.com`? Connect's embed only works there
(K1).
**Q-K3.** Which Connect workspace and who creates HL Central's key (an Admin in Connect)?
**Q-K4.** Do we start with the parts that need nothing from Connect's team (K2, K3), then K1,
K5 and K4?
