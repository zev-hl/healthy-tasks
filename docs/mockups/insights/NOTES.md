# Insights (KPI) page: mockup notes

Files: `kpi-mockup.html` (generated by `build.js`), `kpi-desktop.png` (1440 wide), `kpi-phone.png` (iPhone 13).
The sample data is for 7 people in 3 departments, period "This month" = Nov 1–11 2026, compared with Oct 1–11.

How it differs from the Dashboard: My dashboard and My team show **current counts** (open, overdue, blocked, done) and lists to act on. Insights shows **rates, trends and comparisons**: % on time, cycle time, hours against what's expected, timesheet discipline, goal outcomes and coverage gaps, each against the previous period.

Period switch: This week (Sun–Sat) / This month (to date) / This quarter (to date). Each is compared with the same span of the previous period. Department filter: All, or one department. "My team" scope as decided: Managers see the departments of the duties they hold, Admins see everyone.

## Headline KPIs (8 tiles)

| # | KPI | Definition | Data source | Drills to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On-time completion | OnTime ÷ (OnTime + Late) for tasks completed in the period | `POST /api/reports/due-date` with `statusChangedFrom/To` = period, `statuses:[Completed]`, `departmentIds` → `bucketTotals`. 12-week sparkline: one `POST /api/tasks/query` of the completed rows (`dueAt` vs `statusChangedAt`) bucketed by week on the client | Tasks / due-date report: Completed late in the period |
| 2 | Tasks completed | Count of tasks with status Completed and `statusChangedAt` in the period | `POST /api/tasks/query` (or the `/dashboard` counts) with `statuses:[Completed]` + `statusChangedFrom/To` | Tasks list: Completed in the period |
| 3 | Median cycle time | Median of `statusChangedAt − createdAt` (days) over the tasks completed in the period | The same `tasks/query` rows (`TaskRowDto.createdAt`, `statusChangedAt`). Caveat: `statusChangedAt` is the *last* status change, so a task that was reopened counts from its final completion. Fine as a start. | Tasks: Completed in the period, sorted longest first |
| 4 | Overdue now | Open tasks with a past due date; also shown as % of open and the oldest one's age | `tasks/query` with `overdue:true` (+ total open). **The delta "since Nov 1" needs a backend job**: there's no history of past overdue counts, so a daily snapshot table is needed. Until then, show no delta. | Tasks: Overdue |
| 5 | Hours logged | Σ logged minutes ÷ (8h × weekdays × people) | `POST /api/time/review` (`days[].minutes`, scope team + `departmentId`). Expected hours are computed on the client. Holidays and leave are unknown. | Time › Review for the period |
| 6 | Timesheets submitted | Person-weekdays marked complete ÷ all person-weekdays | `time/review` `days[].completed` | Time › Review, filtered to the days that aren't submitted |
| 7 | Goals met or exceeded | Resolved in the period with resolution Met or Exceeded ÷ resolved in the period | `POST /api/goals/team` (`status`, `resolution`, `resolvedAt`), filtered on the client by `resolvedAt` | Goals › Team goals: Resolved |
| 8 | Review turnaround | Median time from "sent to review" to "reviewed" | **Needs a backend job.** Review events are only in `TaskHistory`, per task; no endpoint lists them across tasks (same as decisions.md backend job #8). Shown as a dashed placeholder | Tasks in Review, waiting longest first |

Delta colors follow meaning: green when the change is good (including *down* for cycle time, overdue and turnaround), red when it's bad.

## Charts and cards

| Card | What it shows | Data source | Click |
|---|---|---|---|
| Completed per week | 12 Sun–Sat weeks as stacked columns: on time (green `#3F9A57`) and late (red `#B42318`). The current week is hatched ("so far"). The pair passes the dataviz CVD validator (ΔE 10.9). | One `tasks/query` (Completed, `statusChangedFrom` = 12 weeks ago) bucketed on the client. | Overdue, by how late | Overdue tasks in age buckets 1–3 / 4–7 / 8–14 / 15–30 / 30+ days, plus "who holds them" chips | `tasks/query overdue:true` rows (`dueAt`, `assignee`) | A bar or chip opens Tasks overdue for that bucket or person |
| By department (table) | People, open (bar), overdue, done, on time %, cycle time, hours logged / expected, timesheets | Same queries grouped by `duty.department` (tasks) and `departmentId` (time). People come from `GET /api/org` / duty holdings | A row scopes the whole page to that department |
| Hours logged by person | Logged vs expected bar with an expected tick. Amber under 75%. A "N days not submitted" pill | `time/review` | A row opens Time › Review for that person |
| Goals | Stage bar Draft → Pending approval → Active → Under review → Resolved (one-hue blue ordinal ramp, validated). Resolution counts. A flag for active goals past their deadline that aren't in review yet | `goals/team` (`status`, `deadline`, `resolution`) | Goals › Team goals filtered by stage |
| Duties nobody holds | Duties with no holder, their open-task count, and how many duties have only one holder | `GET /api/departments` + `GET /api/departments/holdings` (+ open tasks per duty from `tasks/query`). Current state only, no trend | People › Organization |

Performance note: everything above *can* be built from the existing endpoints in the browser (same fallback as backend job #6 for My team). A single `GET /api/insights?period=&departmentId=` aggregate endpoint would be faster and is the recommended build, but it isn't required for a first version.

## Open questions for you

1. **Who sees it?** Admins only, or Managers too (scoped to their departments / downline)? And should Members get a "my own KPIs" version?
2. **Where does it live?** A new sidebar item "Insights" (as mocked, between Dashboard and Tasks), or a third Dashboard tab (My dashboard · My team · Insights)?
3. **Scope rule for Managers:** their departments (as My team) or their supervisor downline (`supervisorId` chain)? The two can differ.
4. **Expected hours:** is 8h × weekdays right for everyone? Are there part-timers, holidays or leave we should know about? (None of these exist in the data today.)
5. **Backend jobs:** OK to add (a) a daily overdue snapshot (for the overdue trend / delta) and (b) a review-events query (for review turnaround)? Or drop those two for v1?
6. **Comparison base:** the same days of the previous period (as mocked), or the full previous period?
7. **Which 8?** Any you'd swap: e.g. "Duty mismatch tasks" (`dutyMismatch` flag), "Recurring tasks generated vs done", or "Unassigned open tasks".
