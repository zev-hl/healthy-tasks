A friendlier Healthy Tasks

Every screen currently in the app, redrawn: foundations, My Day, Tasks, Task detail, Notifications, Users, Profile, and Sign in. Same data model and same features — different structure, hierarchy, and tone. Notes under each frame say what changed and why.

The six statuses and four priorities come straight from TASK_STATUS_LABELS and TASK_PRIORITIES. Overdue is the seventh pill but it is a derived date state, not a status. A three-bar ramp reads priority at a glance and takes less room than the word.

Why
  • The current palette is browser-default blue on cool gray with no accent of its own; warm neutrals plus one green make the app feel like a product rather than a scaffold.
  • toLocaleString() appears on every date in the app. Relative wording first, absolute second, is the single highest-value change per line of code.
  • Raw email addresses are used as person labels in eight places. Avatar plus display name is shorter and scans faster.
